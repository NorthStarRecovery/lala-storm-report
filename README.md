# Lala storm intelligence — operations & BD report

Post-storm operations and business development assessment for Hurricane/Tropical Storm Lala
(Hawaiian Islands, August 2026). Prepared for NorthStar leadership and operations, and for
Executive General Adjusters working the event.

**Live page:** https://northstarrecovery.github.io/lala-storm-report/
**PDF:** https://northstarrecovery.github.io/lala-storm-report/NorthStar-Lala-Ops-BD-Report.pdf

## How to read it

Research cutoff: **August 17, 2026, 08:00 HST.** Superseded by any later official release.

Every figure carries its source and a confidence label (CONFIRMED / CORROBORATED / PRELIMINARY /
SINGLE-SOURCE / DISPUTED) with an as-of timestamp. Snapshots from different hours are never merged,
and conflicting tallies are shown side by side rather than averaged or resolved. Where the record is
silent, the report says so instead of filling the gap.

This is situational intelligence — not a coverage determination, engineering assessment, or legal advice.

## Imagery

No third-party photography is embedded. The cover visual and island relief tiles are illustrative
renderings, not satellite, survey, or event imagery. Verified damage photographs are listed in the
report's imagery registry with credits, license classes, and links to their original publication.
